Privacy Policy

SMS Webhook (Android app com.bestcallerapp.smswebhook) · Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what SMS Webhook ("the app") does with information, published by Best Caller, Bengaluru, India ("we"). In short: the app forwards your SMS only to the web addresses you configure; we do not receive, store or sell your messages.

1. What the app processes, and where it goes

InformationWhyWhere it is storedWhere it is sent
Incoming SMS: sender, text, times, SIM slot and subscriptionTo forward messages that match your rulesOn your phone, in the app's private storageOnly to the webhook URLs you add, when a message matches that webhook
Webhook settings (URLs, rules, headers)To know where and how to forwardOn your phone. Secret header values are encrypted with the Android KeystoreNowhere, unless you export them to a file you choose
Delivery results and logs (status, HTTP code, response, timing)To show delivery history and retry failuresOn your phoneNowhere
Device model and Android version (optional field, off by default)Only if you add the "device" field to a payloadNot storedOnly to your own webhook

The webhook URLs are chosen and controlled by you. What happens to forwarded messages on those servers is governed by whoever operates them (usually you or your organisation).

2. What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server that receives your SMS, settings or delivery data.
  • The app has no account or sign-up, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app does not read your existing SMS inbox, does not send SMS, and does not access contacts or location.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

3. Permissions

  • Receive SMS: to see new SMS as they arrive. Required for the app to work.
  • Internet and network state: to send to your webhooks and wait for a connection.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, run at startup: used by Android's WorkManager to finish deliveries in the background and after a restart.

4. Security

Release versions send data only over HTTPS with certificate verification and do not follow redirects. Secret header values are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. App data is excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfer.

5. Retention and deletion

Messages and logs are kept on your phone according to your retention settings (by default 30 days) and can be deleted at any time in Settings (Clear history, Clear logs, Clear all application data) or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. See how to delete your data.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

We will update this page when the app's handling of information changes, and change the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact and grievance officer

Questions or complaints: smswebhook@bestcallerapp.com. Grievance officer (India): Best Caller, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, at the same email address. We aim to respond within 7 days.
